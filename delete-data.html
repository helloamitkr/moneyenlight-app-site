Delete your MoneyEnlight data

MoneyEnlight by codecloudworld

MoneyEnlight keeps your data only on your phone. There is no MoneyEnlight server account to close — deleting the data on your phone removes it completely.

Delete everything from inside the app

  1. Open MoneyEnlight.
  2. Open the menu (☰) and tap Settings & Horizons.
  3. Under Your Data, tap Delete all my data and confirm.

This erases your profile, investments, profit & loss, targets, settings and attached photos, signs you out and removes the app's access to your Google account. It cannot be undone, so export a backup first if you might want the data later.

Or uninstall the app

Uninstalling MoneyEnlight from your phone also deletes all of its data.

Other copies

  • Backup files you exported are yours — delete them from wherever you saved them.
  • Android backup: to remove the copy Android may keep in your Google account, open your phone's Settings → Google → Backup (names vary by phone), or manage it at drive.google.com/drive/backups.
  • Google access: you can also remove MoneyEnlight's access at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • Crash reports contain no personal data and are kept by Sentry for a limited time and then deleted automatically.
