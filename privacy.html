MoneyEnlight Privacy Policy

Effective 25 November 2026 · Published by codecloudworld

MoneyEnlight is a personal tool for tracking your own investments. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: your data stays on your phone. We do not run a server that stores your portfolio, and we do not sell or share your data.

1. Information stored on your device

Everything you enter is saved only in the app's private storage on your phone:

  • Profile: name, email, phone, city, date of birth, photo, occupation, income bracket, risk profile and investing experience — all optional.
  • Investments: stocks, mutual funds, gold, silver, crypto and other assets you add, their buy prices, quantities, dates, notes and screenshots you attach.
  • Profit & loss, targets and settings you create in the app.

We (the developer) cannot see this information.

2. Google sign-in

If you choose "Sign in with Google", Google shares your account ID, email address, name and profile photo with the app. These are stored on your device only, to show your profile. You can use the app without an account instead.

3. Crash reports

When the app crashes, it can send a crash report to Sentry, our error-tracking provider, so we can fix the problem. A report contains technical details only: the error, the app version, the device model and the Android version. It does not include your name, email, portfolio, screenshots or any data you entered. You can turn crash reports off at any time in Settings & Horizons → Your Data → Send crash reports.

4. Price data

To show current values, the app downloads public prices from:

  • a Google Sheet for Indian stock prices,
  • AMFI and mfapi.in for mutual fund NAVs,
  • open.er-api.com for the USD → INR rate.

These requests do not include your holdings or personal details. Like any website, these services can see your device's IP address when the app connects.

5. Backups

  • Export: Settings & Horizons → Your Data → Export backup creates a backup file. You choose where to save or share it.
  • Android backup: if Android backup is on for your phone, Android may copy the app's data to your own Google account, encrypted, as it does for other apps. You control this in your phone's settings.

6. What we don't do

  • No ads and no advertising IDs.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with anyone.
  • No location, contacts, camera roll scanning or tracking. Photos are used only when you pick one.

7. Deleting your data

Open Settings & Horizons → Your Data → Delete all my data to erase everything the app stores on your phone and disconnect your Google account. Uninstalling the app also removes it. Full steps: Delete your data.

8. Children

MoneyEnlight is meant for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

9. Not financial advice

MoneyEnlight does not give investment, tax or financial advice and is not a SEBI-registered adviser. Prices may be delayed or wrong.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
